About

[ Your Photo ]

I'm a data scientist at Michigan State University, in the Department of Pharmacology and Toxicology, building image analysis pipelines for toxicology research. Most of my day is spent with microscopy images, HPC jobs, and the plumbing that turns raw lab output into something a researcher can actually use.

I came to data through engineering. I studied Manufacturing Engineering at BITS Pilani with a minor in Finance, spent three years as an analyst in EdTech and then a data engineer in healthcare analytics, and finished an MS in Data Science at MSU in 2026. Along the way I did capstones with Henry Ford Health and Meijer, and led undergraduate teams on community data projects through MSU's REI program.

I like problems where the data is messy and the result matters to someone. Healthcare keeps pulling me back.
